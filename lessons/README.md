# Domain lessons

The 14 lessons are grouped into the five AIF-C01 exam domains. The [main guide](../README.md#domain-guide) maps every objective to its lesson section.

## Domain 1: Fundamentals of AI and ML (20%)

| Task | Lesson | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 Explain basic AI concepts and terminologies | [AI and ML concepts](domain-1-ai-ml/01-ai-ml-concepts.md) | AI, ML, deep learning, GenAI, learning methods, data types, fit, and inference |
| 1.2 Identify practical use cases for AI | [AI use cases and service selection](domain-1-ai-ml/02-ai-use-cases.md) | AI fit, ML techniques, managed AI services, traditional ML, FMs, and agents |
| 1.3 Describe the AI/ML development lifecycle | [AI/ML lifecycle, MLOps, and metrics](domain-1-ai-ml/03-lifecycle-mlops-metrics.md) | Data, training, evaluation, deployment, monitoring, MLOps, and business metrics |

[Practice Domain 1](../practice/question-bank.md#domain-1-fundamentals-of-ai-and-ml)
