# AWS Certified AI Practitioner exam overview

Last reviewed: November 3, 2026

AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) checks whether you can recognize AI, machine learning, and generative AI concepts and choose suitable AWS services for business scenarios. AWS describes the target candidate as someone with up to six months of exposure to AI/ML technologies on AWS who uses, but does not necessarily build, AI/ML solutions. The six months is a target-candidate description, not a mandatory prerequisite or eligibility rule.

## Exam facts

| Item | Current detail |
|---|---|
| Exam | AWS Certified AI Practitioner (AIF-C01) |
| Level | Foundational |
| Length | 90 minutes |
| Questions | 65 total: 50 scored and 15 unscored |
| Passing score | 700 on a 100–1,000 scale |
| Question formats | Multiple choice, multiple response, ordering, and matching |
| Scoring | Compensatory: you pass the exam as a whole, not each domain separately |

AWS does not identify the unscored questions. Unanswered questions count as incorrect, and guessing has no penalty.

Confirm scheduling, delivery, language, and price details on the [official certification page](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-ai-practitioner/) before booking.

## Domains and weights

| Domain | Weight | Plain-language focus |
|---|---:|---|
| 1. Fundamentals of AI and ML | 20% | AI terms, learning types, use cases, lifecycle, and classical ML metrics |
| 2. Fundamentals of generative AI | 24% | Foundation models, tokens, context, embeddings, agents, value, limits, and AWS GenAI options |
| 3. Applications of foundation models | 28% | Model selection, prompting, RAG, customization, and evaluation |
| 4. Guidelines for responsible AI | 14% | Bias, fairness, safety, transparency, explainability, and human oversight |
| 5. Security, compliance, and governance | 14% | Access, encryption, privacy, monitoring, audit evidence, and governance |

Domain 3 has the largest weight and builds on the concepts in Domains 1 and 2.

## Skills the exam does and does not test

Expect to:

- distinguish AI, ML, deep learning, generative AI, and agentic AI;
- match a business need to an AI technique or AWS service;
- compare prompting, RAG, fine-tuning, continued pre-training, and training from scratch;
- interpret common model, application, and business metrics;
- identify responsible-AI, privacy, security, and governance concerns;
- choose the best answer from several services that could play some role in a solution.

AWS lists model development, coding, feature engineering implementation, hyperparameter tuning, infrastructure deployment, statistical analysis, and policy implementation outside the target candidate's job tasks.
